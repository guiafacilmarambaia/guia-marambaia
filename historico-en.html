Origin and Urban Occupation

Born from former railway axes and tracks adjacent to lands used by the military, the neighborhood has expanded significantly over the last decades. Urban consolidation occurred with the opening and paving of major commercial and social corridors, such as Rodolfo Chermont Ave, Tavares Bastos Ave, and the iconic Marinha Street.
