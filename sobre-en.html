Urban Guide Explanation

The Marambaia Easy Guide was developed as a digital tool for community, social, and pedagogical utility. The main objective is to catalog essential services, the transport network, educational axes, and the rich cultural heritage of the Marambaia territory, facilitating access to information and promoting integration between residents and local commerce.
